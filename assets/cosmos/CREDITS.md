# assets/cosmos — image credits

All files are real astronomical photographs, resized and recompressed to WebP for this site.
Backdrop photos had their black point pulled to true black and a soft elliptical falloff baked in
(so they blend with `mix-blend-mode: screen`); asteroid cut-outs had the black sky converted to alpha.
Source files were fetched from Wikimedia Commons copies of the original agency releases.
