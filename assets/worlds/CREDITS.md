Changes made:
- all files: downscaled and recompressed.
- saturn.jpg: faint procedural wind streaks (about 10% brightness variation) added so the rotation is visible.
- earth.jpg: saturation raised slightly.
- saturn-ring.webp: the radial ring strip was wrapped into a top-down annulus with its alpha channel (720x720).
